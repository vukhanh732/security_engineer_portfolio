BrainTrain.space

Brain training with a world to come back to. Short maths, memory, focus and pattern challenges meet a 3D guardian campaign. Start playing without an account.

Play BrainTrain ↗

React · TypeScript · Three.js · IndexedDB

Why it exists

I wanted short practice sessions to feel like part of a game world. The redesigned app combines direct skill training with guardian encounters, using a 3D scene and a local progress journal.

Training and campaign

Players can choose Maths, Memory, Focus, Patterns or Mixed training, then set pace, difficulty and question count. Practice is untimed, Sprint is timed and Survival uses health. The campaign adds encounter rules, phases and response windows.

Local-first product decision

React and TypeScript drive the interface; Three.js renders the world. IndexedDB keeps the journey on this browser. There is no account requirement, and cross-device synchronization is outside the current release.

What is released

Two encounters, the Duelist and Illusionist, are playable. The five-guardian path is a roadmap inside the app; Guardians III-V are explicitly unreleased. These screenshots show the current public redesign, replacing the earlier Supabase/Elo version.
